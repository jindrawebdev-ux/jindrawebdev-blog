HTACCESS CHANGE NEEDED — ONE RULE

Your existing .htaccess already handles /blog automatically once blog.php
exists at your site root (it works exactly like /about -> about.php).
Nothing to add for the landing page.

Individual articles (/blog/some-slug) need ONE new rule, because there's
no physical some-slug.php file for each article — they're all served
dynamically through article.php.

Open your existing root .htaccess and add this rule BEFORE the block
labeled "# Serve .php files when clean URLs are requested" (that's the
generic catch-all — your new rule needs to run first so it doesn't fall
through to a 404):

    # Blog article pages: /blog/{slug} -> /article.php?slug={slug}
    RewriteRule ^blog/([a-z0-9\-]+)/?$ /article.php?slug=$1 [L,QSA]

So the relevant section of your .htaccess should read, in order:

    RewriteCond %{THE_REQUEST} \s/+(.+?)\.php[\s?] [NC]
    RewriteCond %{REQUEST_URI} !^/send-contact\.php$ [NC]
    RewriteRule ^(.+?)\.php$ /$1 [R=301,L]

    # Blog article pages: /blog/{slug} -> /article.php?slug={slug}
    RewriteRule ^blog/([a-z0-9\-]+)/?$ /article.php?slug=$1 [L,QSA]

    # Serve .php files when clean URLs are requested
    RewriteCond %{REQUEST_FILENAME} !-d
    RewriteCond %{REQUEST_FILENAME} !-f
    RewriteCond %{DOCUMENT_ROOT}/$1.php -f
    RewriteRule ^(.+)$ /$1.php [L]

    ...(rest unchanged)

Nothing else in your .htaccess needs to change.
